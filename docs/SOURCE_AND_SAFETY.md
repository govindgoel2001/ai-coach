# Source, rights, privacy, and high-stakes guardrails

## Sources
Use public pages, your own notes/files, licensed content, or content you are otherwise authorized to process. Do not use this repo to bypass paywalls, DRM, authentication, or platform access controls.

## Copyright
Store citations and distilled frameworks rather than redistributing full books or large transcripts. Keep any quoted excerpts short and necessary.

## Public figures
The generated coach must not claim to be the public figure, imply endorsement, or fabricate quotes. "Inspired by documented public work" is the right framing.

## Health / finance / legal
These coach modes are educational decision-support. They are not a doctor, investment adviser, lawyer, or other licensed professional. Preserve uncertainty, show evidence, and use human/professional review where stakes are high.

## Personal data
User data belongs in ignored `coaches/*/data/` by default. Avoid committing health, financial, CRM, or private notes to Git. Prefer read-only API scopes and local processing when possible.
